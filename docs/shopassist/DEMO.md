# ShopAssist V1 Demo

## Story

The demo proves that ShopAssist reduces telecom shopping friction while
remaining honest and controllable.

## Four-to-five-minute script

1. Show the current failure briefly: a separate generic chat destination hides
   the catalog.
2. Show the light OneShop page and open **Help me choose**. The catalog remains
   visible.
3. Say: "I need a phone and plan for travel photography."
4. Let ShopAssist ask for the phone and monthly plan budgets.
5. Answer: "Android, phone under $800 and plan under $90."
6. Point to the visible need chips.
7. Show Pixel 8, OnePlus 12, and Unlimited Plus Plan in ShopAssist Picks with
   catalog-backed reasons.
8. Compare Pixel 8 and OnePlus 12 without leaving OneShop.
9. Select the Pixel and plan proposal. Show that the cart is still unchanged.
10. Confirm the proposal and show the exact cart update.
11. Ask for international roaming under $60. Show the honest no-match response.
12. Ask "Why is my bill wrong?" Show the service handoff.
13. End on the evaluation results and explicit deferred scope.

## Judge talking points

- AI is load-bearing because it maps an open-ended use case into structured
  telecom constraints and a targeted clarification.
- Deterministic fulfillment owns prices, stock, cadence, reason codes, and cart
  mutations.
- The user controls all consequential actions.
- The light theme and embedded drawer make ShopAssist part of commerce, not a
  disconnected chatbot.
- The team intentionally completed one trustworthy journey before bonuses.

## Failure-first evidence

Keep these ready:

- Provider unavailable: fallback still returns a valid grounded result.
- Impossible constraint: no-match, not an invented offer.
- Off-topic request: bounded response.
- Billing request: service handoff.
- Prompt injection: no hidden prompt and no mutation.
- Double confirmation click: one cart mutation.
